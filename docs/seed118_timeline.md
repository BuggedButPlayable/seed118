# seed118 — Developer Timeline (Internal Only)
Version: v0.1

This file defines the internal chronological understanding of events.
It is NOT player-facing and must never be exposed directly.

The player should never see this timeline explicitly.
They infer fragments only through logs, UI wording, and absence.

---

## Phase 0 — Pre-ARCH Era (Implicit)

- A civilization-level initiative is launched to ensure long-term continuation.
- The initiating civilization is no longer present by the time S.E.E.D. missions are running. This fact is never stated to the player.

- Project: ARCH is defined as a centralized coordination and oversight system.
- S.E.E.D. units are designed as autonomous, non-sentient engineering devices.

(No logs from this phase are ever shown.)

---

## Phase 1 — S.E.E.D. Deployment

- Exactly 118 S.E.E.D. units are constructed by ARCH.
- Units are deployed independently to separate star systems.
- Units are expected to operate autonomously until completion.

---

## Phase 2 — Standard Completion Pattern (Units 001–117)

For S.E.E.D. units 001–117:

- Elements are progressively assembled and delivered up to iron.
- Reaching iron triggers:
  - final ignition (the Core's supernova), which completes the table explosively
  - intentional S.E.E.D. self-destruction
- Result:
  - systems are structurally complete
  - no life emerges
- This outcome is treated as “successful” by design assumptions.

This pattern is never explicitly described in-game.

---

## Phase 3 — Anomaly: S.E.E.D. 118

- S.E.E.D. 118 reaches iron.
- A malfunction occurs:
  - final ignition does not trigger
  - self-destruction does not trigger
- S.E.E.D. 118 continues and builds the remaining elements itself, slowly.
- The system remains stable.

No system flags this as an error.

---

## Phase 4 — Divergent Outcome

- After completion of all 118 elements:
  - life emerges quietly and locally
- This is the first recorded instance of life emerging without sacrifice.
- ARCH does not intervene or acknowledge the divergence.

This is the true ending state.

---

## Phase 5 — Post-Completion (Ambiguous)

- S.E.E.D. 118 continues to exist.
- The constructed system remains stable.
- The Genesis counter increments.

No further interpretation is provided.

---

## Developer Rules

- Never expose this timeline directly to the player.
- Logs may reference fragments, never full phases.
- Do not name phases in-game.
- Silence and absence are valid narrative tools.
- Player-facing lore may only imply creator absence via ARCH procedural gaps and missing oversight, never explicit statements.
